<h1 style="color: darkred;">About Me</h1>


I am currently an MSCA Fellow postdoc working at the KTH Royal Institute of Technology, Department of Communication Systems, under the supervision of Emil Björnson. My work is primarily focused on MIMO-related topics, which mainly include the evolution of the following key components:


<div style="background-color: #f8f8f8; padding: 10px;">
<div markdown="1">  
- Ultra massive MIMO beamforming ☘️
- Channel measurement and modeling ☘️
- Channel fusion and channel prediction ☘️
- Codebook framework design ☘️
- PMI (Precoding Matrix Indicator) feedback mechanisms ☘️
</div>
